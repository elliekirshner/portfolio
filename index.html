OctoBot: soft robot with touch sensing

2025–2026

An octopus-inspired soft robot that identifies what it is holding by touch alone, with no onboard cameras.

What I did

  • Built the robot on my own, with 4 independently actuated arms
  • Deployed an onboard object classifier

Key decisions

  • 95A TPU segments → bend with minimal effort and are 3D printable
  • Tendon + servo winch → simple control with enough force to bend each arm
  • A single embedded pressure sensor → touch sensing in a low-cost soft robot
  • Random Forest → runs on an ESP32 and suits small datasets

Results

  • Over 90% accuracy by touch alone
  • Published at SAB 2026 (first author) · presented at SAB 2026
  • IRT Award 2026 · presented at the UROP & IRT Symposium 2026

Keeping rigid electronics from binding

  • Problem: rigid sensor connections broke the sensor or blocked movement
  • Optimized segment geometry → smooth, interference-free grasping
  • Custom mounts → a clear path of travel for rigid parts

CuttleBot: robot shell design

2024

A cuttlefish-inspired robot that uses reinforcement learning to learn to avoid predators and approach prey.

What I did

  • Designed the 3D-printable, semi-transparent shell that acts as the robot's camouflaging "skin"
  • Gathered data and wrote up the robot's mechanical capabilities for the paper

Key decisions

  • Semi-transparent shell → hides the electronics while spreading LED light evenly
  • Internal ribs → hold the LED strips
  • Rim tabs + front opening → mount to the base and clear the camera and claw

Results

  • Shell displayed camouflage, blanching, and a hypnotic pattern
  • Published at SAB 2024 (co-author)
